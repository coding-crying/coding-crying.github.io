About Me

AI Researcher, Entrepreneur, and Innovator bridging technology and real-world impact

Who Am I?

I'm an AI architect and researcher with a BSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Groningen, specializing in building decentralized, privacy-preserving AI systems. I'm passionate about challenging big tech monopolies by creating open-source alternatives that put users back in control of their data and digital experiences.

My recent work demonstrates this commitment: I developed a comprehensive Neo4j-powered language tutoring platform featuring real-time voice interaction and intelligent memory modeling, built a federated learning system for privacy-preserving social media recommendations, and created local advertisement content generation pipelines that operate entirely offline. Each project represents a step toward AI decentralization and user sovereignty.

Beyond AI development, I bring diverse technical experience from cognitive modeling research using ACT-R architecture to hardware innovation holding U.S. Patent #11,220,244 for recreational device braking systems. My background includes founding tech consulting businesses, product design with rapid prototyping, and three years of international experience living and studying in Europe.

I believe the future of AI lies in transparent, user-controlled systems that enhance human capabilities rather than replace them. Whether through federated learning, local deployment, or open-source development, I'm committed to building technology that serves humanity's interests while maintaining individual privacy and autonomy.
